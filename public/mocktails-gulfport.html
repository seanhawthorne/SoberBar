On the list

  • Call Me An Ube — Ritual gin alt, ube, coconut cream, key lime, pineapple
  • Hot Mami — reposado tequila alt, jalapeño, lime, pineapple ginger, chili
  • Jinn & Juniper — gin alt, cucumber, juniper, and ashwagandha
  • Bee’s Bouquet — gin alt, fresh lemon, honey, and lavender
  • Moonlit Meringue — gin alt, alcohol-free limoncello, egg white, lavender bitters — cloud-soft
  • Espresso Martini — reposado tequila alt, cold brew espresso, agave, fig and walnut bitters
  • Lilikoi

Alcohol-free beers: Run Wild IPA, Guinness 0.0, and Heineken Zero. Beech Kombucha is a bright, sparkling fermented tea on draft. See drink photos on the menu.
