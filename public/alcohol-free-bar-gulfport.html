An alcohol-free bar in Gulfport

Sober Bar & Kitchen is a bar on Beach Boulevard in Gulfport. The drinks are alcohol-free, the kitchen is New Orleans-inspired, and the doors open at 7am every day.

How a visit goes

The address is 3062 Beach Blvd S. Morning is the coffee hour, with free, fast WiFi if you are working. See coffee and breakfast. From there the same room turns to alcohol-free cocktails, alcohol-free beer, and kombucha, and the kitchen stays on from open to close. Every cocktail’s ingredients are in the mocktails guide. The full plates are on the menu.

Live jazz every Sunday with the Western Gypsies. Brunch is 10am–1pm. Book that service on the jazz brunch page. Any other day, use the button below. Hours are Sunday through Thursday 7am–10pm, and Friday and Saturday 7am–midnight.
