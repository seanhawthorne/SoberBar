A Perfect Weekend in Gulfport, Florida

July 2026 • Sober Bar, Gulfport, FL

Gulfport is the kind of place where a single weekend can include a farmers market, a waterfront sunset, a gallery opening, and one of the best zero-proof cocktail bars in Tampa Bay.

If you're planning a weekend in Gulfport, here's how to do it right.

Saturday Morning: Local Shops and Coffee

Start by browsing the local shops and cafés along Beach Boulevard. Grab a cortado or cold brew at Sober Bar and plan your day.

Afternoon: The Pier and the Beach

Walk down to the Gulfport Pier. It's one of the most relaxed waterfront spots in Pinellas County, and it's free. Bring a book, take photos, or just watch the water.

Evening: Dinner + Mocktails

Gulfport has solid dinner options — seafood, Italian, casual waterfront spots. After dinner, head to Sober Bar for a nightcap that won't slow you down tomorrow. Try the Smoked Old Fashioned or a Kava Cloud.

First Friday / Third Saturday: Art Walk

Gulfport Art Walk is one of the best neighborhood events in Tampa Bay. Galleries open, vendors line the street, and there's live music. Start or end at Sober Bar.

Sunday: Coffee and a Slow Exit

Sleep in, then come back for espresso and a quiet morning before the drive home. No hangover required.

Make Sober Bar Your Weekend Base

We're open Tuesday through Sunday at 3062 Beach Blvd S in Gulfport. Coffee by day, cocktails by night.
