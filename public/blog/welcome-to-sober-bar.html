Welcome to Sober Bar: Gulfport's First Alcohol-Free Cocktail Bar

July 2026 • Sober Bar, Gulfport, FL

There aren't many places where you can walk in, order a Smoked Old Fashioned, and know for certain there's no alcohol in the glass. Sober Bar is one of them.

We're Gulfport's first fully alcohol-free cocktail bar and coffee house, located at 3062 Beach Blvd S. Whether you're sober, sober-curious, pregnant, driving, or just want a great drink without the hangover, this bar was built for you.

What We Serve

By day, we're a coffee house pouring specialty espresso, cold brew, and café favorites. By night, we flip into a candlelit speakeasy serving craft zero-proof cocktails, kava drinks, kratom seltzer, and NA beer.

Our most popular drinks include:

  • Smoked Old Fashioned — zero-proof bourbon alternative, bitters, smoke, orange peel
  • Espresso Martini — bold, foamy, no crash
  • Kava Cloud — botanical calm in cocktail form
  • Melo Mule — ginger-forward and refreshing

Why Gulfport?

Gulfport is independent, artsy, and community-first. It didn't have a dedicated alcohol-free nightlife option, and it felt like the perfect place to build one. We're walking distance from the pier, the Tuesday Fresh Market, and Art Walk.

What "Full Ritual, Zero Proof" Means

We believe the ritual of a cocktail matters: the glassware, the garnish, the shake, the pour. We just skip the alcohol. The result is a drink that feels like a real night out, minus the regrets.

Come Say Hi

Sober Bar is open Tuesday through Sunday at 3062 Beach Blvd S, Gulfport, FL. Bring a friend who "doesn't drink" and let us change their mind.
