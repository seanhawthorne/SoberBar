Behind the Bar: How We Build a Zero-Proof Cocktail

July 2026 • Sober Bar, Gulfport, FL

A great zero-proof cocktail isn't just juice in a fancy glass. At Sober Bar, we build every drink with the same precision as a traditional cocktail — because the ritual matters.

Start with Balance

Every good cocktail, alcoholic or not, needs balance: sweet, sour, bitter, and aromatic. We use fresh citrus, house-made syrups, shrubs, and bitters to hit all four notes.

Add Complexity

Alcohol carries flavor and adds bite. Without it, we layer complexity with:

  • Non-alcoholic spirits — botanical alternatives to gin, whiskey, and tequila
  • Bitters — a few dashes transforms a drink
  • Shrubs — vinegar-based syrups for depth and tang
  • Smoke — adds drama and aroma
  • Fresh herbs and garnishes — rosemary, citrus peel, dehydrated fruit

Build the Ritual

We shake, stir, strain, smoke, and garnish like any craft bar. The glassware matters. The ice matters. The pour matters. When everything comes together, the drink feels complete.

Example: Smoked Old Fashioned

We start with a zero-proof bourbon alternative, add aromatic and orange bitters, a touch of simple syrup, and a big cube of ice. Then we trap smoke under a glass cloche and lift it tableside. The result looks, smells, and tastes like the real thing.
