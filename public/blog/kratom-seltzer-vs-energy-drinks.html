What Is Kratom Seltzer?

Kratom seltzer is sparkling water infused with kratom, a plant native to Southeast Asia. People use kratom for a gentle energy lift, focus, or relaxation, depending on the strain and amount.

How Is It Different From Energy Drinks?

Responsible Use

Kratom affects everyone differently. Start with a small amount, avoid mixing with other substances, and don't drive if you feel impaired. If you have health conditions or take medications, talk to your doctor first.
