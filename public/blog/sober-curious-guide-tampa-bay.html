The Sober-Curious Guide to Tampa Bay

July 2026 • Sober Bar, Gulfport, FL

The sober-curious movement isn't about quitting alcohol forever. It's about choosing not to drink in moments when you don't want to — and having places that make that choice easy.

Tampa Bay has more sober-friendly options than most people realize. Here's a guide to the best of them.

Sober Bar — Gulfport

Gulfport's first fully alcohol-free cocktail bar and coffee house. Zero-proof cocktails, kava, kratom seltzer, espresso, and a real bar atmosphere. Open Tuesday through Sunday.

The Phoenix Tampa

A sober, active community with free fitness and social events. Great for people in recovery or anyone building a healthier social life.

barNone

A St. Pete-based social group for people who want to have fun without alcohol. They host events, meetups, and collaborations around Tampa Bay.

Outdoor Activities

St. Pete has beaches, parks, and the Pinellas Trail. A morning at Fort De Soto or an evening walk on the Gulfport Pier is a perfect alcohol-free activity.

Coffee Shops & Cafés

Many Tampa Bay coffee shops now stay open late and offer elevated non-alcoholic options. Sober Bar bridges the gap between café culture and nightlife.

How to Start

If you're sober-curious, start small. Pick one night out at an alcohol-free spot. Notice how you sleep, how you feel the next day, and whether you still had fun. Most people are surprised.

Start Your Sober-Curious Night in Gulfport

Sober Bar is open Tuesday through Sunday at 3062 Beach Blvd S. Try a zero-proof cocktail and see what the movement is about.
