Breakfast and coffee in Gulfport, FL

Espresso, cold brew, chai, and a New Orleans-inspired breakfast from 7am, with free, fast WiFi.

WiFiBreakfastQuestions

A coffee shop with free WiFi

Sober Bar & Kitchen is Gulfport’s coffee shop in the morning and a cocktail bar later. Coffee, breakfast, and free, fast WiFi from 7am. Bring a laptop or just come for breakfast.

Start the morning with coffee, daylight, and free, fast WiFi. Reserve a table if you want one held, especially once the room shifts toward lunch and evening.

3062 Beach Blvd S, Gulfport, FL 33707 · (813) 586-1660
Sunday–Thursday 7am–10pm · Friday–Saturday 7am–midnight. Free, fast WiFi. Full menu from open to close.

Breakfast from 7am

Breakfast sandwiches, bagels, and the rest of the kitchen menu start at open. Call for current prices.

  • Cortado — espresso with a small pour of steamed milk
  • Cappuccino — espresso, steamed milk, and foam
  • Americano — espresso and hot water
  • Café au Lait — hot coffee and steamed milk
  • Latte — espresso and steamed milk
  • Mocha — espresso, chocolate, and steamed milk
  • Kyoto Cold Brew — slow-dripped cold brew
  • Nitro Cold Brew — cold brew poured on nitro
  • Chai Latte — spiced chai with steamed milk
  • The Classic Morning Melt — English muffin, egg, American cheese, and bacon, sausage, or ham
  • Beach Blvd Bodega — everything bagel, egg, American cheese, choice of meat
  • The White Rabbit — plain bagel, egg, brie, fig spread, prosciutto, hot honey
  • The Mad Hatter — English muffin or bagel, egg, everything bacon, choice of meat, provolone, tomato, spinach, pesto
  • Breakfast Muffuletta — toasted focaccia, pesto cream cheese, egg, sausage, bacon, smoked provolone
  • Big Easy Breakfast Flatbread — pesto cream cheese, mozzarella, sausage, bacon, egg, hot honey

The kitchen also keeps the all-day plates on from open to close, including The Muffuletta and the plant-based Garden District Muffuletta (vegan pesto, olive tapenade, roasted peppers, sourdough). Tell us about allergies before you order. The full list is on the menu.

Questions

What time does breakfast start?

Every day at 7am. Sunday through Thursday the room is open until 10pm. Friday and Saturday it is open until midnight. The full menu runs from open to close.

Do I need a reservation for coffee?

Reserve online if you want a table held. For 7+, call (813) 586-1660.

Can I work from here during the day?

Yes. Free, fast WiFi, seats, and espresso from 7am.
