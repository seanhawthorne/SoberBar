Breakfast and coffee in Gulfport

Espresso, cold brew, and a New Orleans-inspired breakfast from 7am. Walk-ins are welcome.

The espresso bar is open with the doors. Free Wi-Fi if you are working. The full menu runs open to close.

Coffee

  • Cortado — Espresso with a small pour of steamed milk
  • Cappuccino — Espresso, steamed milk, and a thick cap of foam
  • Americano — Espresso and hot water
  • Café au Lait — Hot coffee and steamed milk — a New Orleans classic
  • Latte — Espresso and steamed milk, smooth and creamy
  • Mocha — Espresso, chocolate, and steamed milk
  • Kyoto Cold Brew — Slow-dripped with a clean, smooth finish
  • Nitro Cold Brew — Cascading nitro pour, naturally creamy
  • Chai Latte — Spiced chai with steamed milk

Breakfast

  • The Classic Morning Melt — English muffin, egg, American cheese, bacon, sausage, or ham
  • The Beach Blvd Bodega — Everything bagel, egg, American cheese, choice of meat
  • The White Rabbit — Plain bagel, egg, brie, fig spread, prosciutto, hot honey
  • The Mad Hatter — English muffin or bagel, egg, everything bacon, choice of meat, provolone, tomato, spinach, pesto
  • Breakfast Muffuletta — Toasted focaccia, pesto cream cheese, egg, sausage, bacon, smoked provolone
  • Hot Honey Breakfast Flatbread — Pesto cream cheese, mozzarella, sausage, bacon, egg, hot honey drizzle
  • Bagel & Cream Cheese — Plain or everything — house ube, fig & honey, or vegan spreads
  • Yogurt Parfait — Greek yogurt, raw honey, mixed berries, Ryker's Granola

We serve non-alcoholic drinks. Some NA beers contain trace alcohol below 0.5% ABV.
