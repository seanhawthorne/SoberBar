The coffee shop

Sober Bar & Kitchen is Gulfport’s coffee shop in the morning and a cocktail bar later. The espresso bar is open from 7am. Order at the bar, take a table, and stay if you are working. The room has free Wi-Fi and seats for a laptop as well as breakfast.

  • Cortado — espresso with a small pour of steamed milk
  • Cappuccino — espresso, steamed milk, and foam
  • Americano — espresso and hot water
  • Café au Lait — hot coffee and steamed milk
  • Latte — espresso and steamed milk
  • Mocha — espresso, chocolate, and steamed milk
  • Kyoto Cold Brew — slow-dripped, served cold
  • Nitro Cold Brew — cascading nitro pour
  • Chai Latte — spiced chai with steamed milk

We serve non-alcoholic drinks. Some NA beers contain trace alcohol below 0.5% ABV.

Breakfast from 7am

Breakfast sandwiches, bagels, and the rest of the kitchen menu start at open. Nothing on this page has a published price; ask in the room or call before you come if you need one.

  • The Classic Morning Melt — English muffin, egg, American cheese, and bacon, sausage, or ham
  • The Beach Blvd Bodega — everything bagel, egg, American cheese, choice of meat
  • The White Rabbit — plain bagel, egg, brie, fig spread, prosciutto, hot honey
  • The Mad Hatter — English muffin or bagel, egg, everything bacon, choice of meat, provolone, tomato, spinach, pesto
  • Breakfast Muffuletta — toasted focaccia, pesto cream cheese, egg, sausage, bacon, smoked provolone
  • Hot Honey Breakfast Flatbread — pesto cream cheese, mozzarella, sausage, bacon, egg, hot honey
  • Bagel & Cream Cheese — plain or everything, with house ube, fig and honey, or vegan spreads
  • Yogurt Parfait — Greek yogurt, raw honey, mixed berries, Ryker’s Granola

The kitchen also keeps the all-day plates on from open to close, including The Muffuletta and the plant-based Garden District Muffuletta (vegan pesto, olive tapenade, roasted peppers, sourdough). Gluten-free options are available; tell us about allergies before you order. The full list is on the menu.

Working here

Mornings are the quiet hour: coffee, daylight, and free Wi-Fi. You do not need a reservation for a laptop and a cortado. Reserve a table if you want one held, especially once the room shifts toward lunch and evening.

Questions

What time does breakfast start?

Every day at 7am. Sunday through Thursday the room is open until 10pm. Friday and Saturday it is open until 12am. The full menu runs from open to close.

Do I need a reservation for coffee?

No. Walk-ins are welcome. Reserve a table when you want the seat kept.

Is there Wi-Fi?

Yes. Free Wi-Fi. Mornings and afternoons are set up for working as well as eating.

Can drinks contain trace alcohol?

We serve non-alcoholic drinks. Some NA beers contain trace alcohol below 0.5% ABV.
