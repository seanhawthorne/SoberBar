Best Non-Alcoholic & Mocktail Bars in Florida

The sober-curious movement is real, and Florida is finally getting spaces that take alcohol-free drinking seriously. Here's where to find real craft and a real night out.

This list only includes venues that do not serve alcohol. A regular bar with a mocktail on the menu is not on it.

Sober Bar

3062 Beach Blvd S, Gulfport, FL 33707 · Sunday–Thursday 7am–10pm · Friday–Saturday 7am–midnight · sipsoberbar.com · @sipsoberbar

Sober Bar is Gulfport's alcohol-free cocktail bar and coffee house. Mornings bring specialty espresso, house-made Kyoto cold brew, and a New Orleans-inspired kitchen. The full menu runs from open to close. After dark the room shifts to a candlelit speakeasy serving craft alcohol-free cocktails and NA beer. We serve non-alcoholic drinks. Some NA beers contain trace alcohol below 0.5% ABV. Try the Call Me an Ube or the Jinn & Juniper.

Why This List Exists

Sober Bar was built because the ritual of going out matters — whether you're sober, sober-curious, the designated driver, pregnant, in recovery, or just taking a night off. We keep this guide updated because the more great alcohol-free options Florida has, the easier it is for everyone to choose clarity without staying home.

Planning a trip through Florida? Start at Sober Bar & Kitchen in Gulfport, FL. The bar is open daily at 7am. See the menu and Sunday jazz brunch in Gulfport.
