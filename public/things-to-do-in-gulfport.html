Things to do in Gulfport

The pier, the beach, and a table on Beach Boulevard.

Waterfront

  • Williams Pier — The Walter and Bert Williams Fishing Pier, at 54th Street South and Shore Boulevard. Free admission, with fishing access, benches, and lighting.
  • Gulfport Beach — City beach on Shore Boulevard between 54th and 58th Streets, open 4 a.m. to midnight.

Eat

  • O'Maddy's Bar & Grille — Waterfront dining at 5405 Shore Blvd S. Open daily 11 a.m. to midnight, with live bands.

Shop

  • Gulfport Night Market — First Friday and third Saturday, 5–9 p.m., along Beach Boulevard.
  • Gulfport Tuesday Fresh Market — A year-round Tuesday market on Beach Boulevard, 9 a.m.–2 p.m.
  • Gulfport Beach Bazaar — Coastal gifts and souvenirs on Beach Boulevard.
