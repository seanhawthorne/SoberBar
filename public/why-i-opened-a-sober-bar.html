Why I opened a sober bar

Gulfport had plenty of nights out. None of them were for people skipping the drink.

I kept asking a simple question: why does going out have to come with alcohol?

Gulfport already had the art walk, the night market, and good food. Skip the drink and your choices were a coffee shop or a sad soda at someone else’s bar.

So I built the bar I wanted to walk into.

The kitchen is New Orleans-inspired. The drinks are built like the cocktails you already order. The room does the rest of the convincing.

People walk in skeptical. Then they taste a Call Me an Ube and stay for the room.

Who it’s for

Designated drivers. Pregnant friends. People in recovery. People who simply don’t feel like it tonight. They still want the night out.

The hard part wasn’t the recipes. It was proving a sober bar could still feel like a bar.
