Why I opened a sober bar

A Gulfport bar for the night out, without the hangover.

The idea started with a simple question: why does going out have to come with alcohol?

Gulfport already had art walks, night markets, and good food. If you were not drinking, the options were a coffee shop or a soda at a regular bar.

We didn’t want a juice bar. We wanted a bar.

Sober Bar & Kitchen at 3062 Beach Blvd S, Gulfport, FL 33707 is a coffee house by day and a cocktail bar by night. Call (813) 586-1660. The kitchen is New Orleans-inspired. The drinks are built like craft cocktails. We serve non-alcoholic drinks. Some NA beers contain trace alcohol below 0.5% ABV. Non-alcoholic cocktails near St. Pete are on that list.

People walk in skeptical. Then they see the room, taste a Call Me an Ube, and stay.

Who comes in

Designated drivers. Pregnant guests. People in recovery. People who just do not feel like drinking tonight. The common thread is choice.

The hardest part was not the drinks. It was convincing people a sober bar could still feel like a bar.
