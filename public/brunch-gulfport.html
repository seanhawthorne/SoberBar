Brunch in Gulfport

Sunday brunch under the chandelier, with live jazz in the room. Book online from 10am to 1pm.

CallReserve Sunday brunch

Sunday brunch at Sober Bar & Kitchen is the midday table on Beach Boulevard. You sit in the lounge: curved booths under a chandelier, the bar in the same room, and the porch just outside when you want air. Doors open at 7am, so coffee can come before the brunch window. Daytime outside that window is walk-in.

The brunch you can reserve is Sunday, 10am–1pm. Live jazz plays every Sunday brunch. Call (813) 586-1660 for this Sunday’s jazz time. No band or start time is published.

If you came for the music, start with Sunday jazz.

Gulfport borders St. Petersburg. The address is 3062 Beach Blvd S, Gulfport, FL 33707. When the table is done, the pier and the beach are a short walk.

The kitchen is New Orleans-inspired. Brunch is not a separate card. The full menu runs open to close, including Sunday.

Online booking is for parties of 1–6. For 7 or more, call (813) 586-1660.

A larger gathering is private events and group dining. Tell us the date and the headcount.
