The Cocktails

Full ritual,
zero proof.

Every drink is built like it belongs on a top-shelf menu — layered, garnished, and worth slowing down for. NA spirits, fresh-pressed juice, real craft.

Each signature pour is the work of one of our own bartenders, built on house syrups and fresh-pressed juice. Ask about adding functional botanicals to any beverage.

Honest pour: our cocktails are built on non-alcoholic spirits. Some NA beers carry trace alcohol, under 0.5% ABV. Ask us anything.

Questions

Before you come through.

Can drinks contain trace alcohol?

We serve non-alcoholic drinks. Some NA beers contain trace alcohol below 0.5% ABV.

What are your hours?

Open daily. Sunday through Thursday 7 am – 10 pm, Friday and Saturday 7 am – midnight. Coffee and breakfast start at 7 am; the full menu runs open to close.

Can I work here during the day?

Absolutely. Mornings and afternoons are coffee-house energy — fast WiFi, good seats, great espresso. The room shifts to bar energy as the sun goes down.

Do you serve food all day?

Yes. Breakfast sandwiches, bagels, flatbreads, muffulettas, and shareable plates from open to close — pretzel bites at 11 pm on a Saturday are encouraged.

Do you host private events or buyouts?

Yes. Sober Bar hosts baby showers, book clubs, office happy hours, recovery milestones, sober-curious meetups, and private celebrations — coffee, alcohol-free cocktails, NA beer, kombucha, and the full menu from open to close. We serve non-alcoholic drinks. Some NA beers contain trace alcohol below 0.5% ABV.

Do you take walk-ins?

Yes. Walk-ins are welcome every day we are open. Reserve a table if you want one held, especially on Friday and Saturday nights.
