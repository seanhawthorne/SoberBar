Before you visit

Is Sober Bar an alcohol-free bar?

Yes. Sober Bar & Kitchen is an alcohol-free bar and café. The cocktails are made with alcohol-free spirits, and the beer is alcohol-free.

Do you serve kava?

Yes. Kava is on the menu, alongside alcohol-free cocktails, coffee and a New Orleans kitchen. See kava in Gulfport.

Where is Sober Bar?

3062 Beach Blvd S in Gulfport, Florida, minutes from downtown St. Petersburg and St. Pete Beach.

Is there live music?

Yes. Live jazz every Sunday with the Western Gypsies during brunch, 10am to 1pm. See Sunday Jazz Brunch.

Can I host a private event?

Yes. Showers, birthdays, office gatherings, and recovery milestones. Send the date and headcount through private events.

What are your hours?

Open daily. Sunday through Thursday 7am–10pm, Friday and Saturday 7am–midnight.

Can I work from here during the day?

Yes. Free, fast WiFi, seats, and espresso from 7am.

Do you serve food all day?

Yes. Breakfast, flatbreads, muffulettas, and shareable plates from open to close.

Do I need a reservation?

Booking holds your table. Book online for 1–6. For 7+, call (813) 586-1660.
