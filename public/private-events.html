Why alcohol-free events work

  • Welcoming. A social setting for anyone choosing an alcohol-free outing.
  • Memorable. Guests remember the drinks and the atmosphere, not the hangover.
  • Productive. Corporate groups stay sharp and conversational.
  • Flexible. Start with your occasion and build a gathering around it.

What guests drink

Our bar program uses non-alcoholic spirits, fresh juices, bitters, shrubs, and house syrups. We serve non-alcoholic drinks. Some NA beers contain trace alcohol below 0.5% ABV. Popular builds include the Call Me an Ube, Jinn & Juniper, and Satsuma-Basil Smash. We can design a custom cocktail for your event or brand.

Gatherings we host

  • Showers. Baby showers and bridal showers, with coffee, alcohol-free cocktails, and the kitchen menu.
  • Birthdays. A reserved section or the full room, depending on the headcount.
  • Office gatherings. Happy hours and team time without a liquor bill.
  • Recovery milestones. A celebration that does not put a drink in front of the guest of honor.
