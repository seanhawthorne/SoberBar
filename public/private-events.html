Group dining

Come for dinner as a group, or for Sunday brunch. Online booking covers parties of 1–6: dinner from 4–9:30pm, and Sunday brunch from 10am–1pm. Daytime is walk-in. For 7 or more, call (813) 586-1660. The online link holds a table for that party size. It does not hold the room.

Reserve Sunday brunch when the group is coming at midday.

Private events

Showers, birthdays, office gatherings, recovery milestones, and book clubs use the same lounge and the same porch. Tell us the date and headcount and we’ll confirm what fits. Menus and pricing are in the quote. An inquiry does not reserve the date.

Sunday jazz brunch is the same room, not a separate venue.
