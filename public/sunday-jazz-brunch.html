What Sunday is

Live jazz every Sunday brunch. The kitchen makes a New Orleans-inspired brunch menu for Sundays, and the doors are already open at 7am if you want coffee first. Walk-ins are welcome. A reservation is the way to hold a table while the music is on.

The start time is not on this page yet. That is intentional until it is confirmed. Use Reserve a table, or call (813) 586-1660, and we will give you the time for the Sunday you want.

What you can order

The full menu runs from open to close, including on Sunday. Confirmed plates include The White Rabbit (plain bagel, egg, brie, fig spread, prosciutto, hot honey) and the plant-based Garden District Muffuletta. Coffee starts with espresso, Kyoto cold brew, and chai. Later, the bar pours alcohol-free cocktails, including Call Me an Ube.

We serve non-alcoholic drinks. Some NA beers contain trace alcohol below 0.5% ABV. The posted list is the menu. Coffee and breakfast has the morning dishes in one place.
