Why Sober Bar?

Sober Bar & Kitchen is an alcohol-free cocktail bar on Beach Boulevard in Gulfport, featured by FOX 13, I Love the Burg, St. Pete Rising, and The Gabber. We serve non-alcoholic drinks. Some NA beers contain trace alcohol below 0.5% ABV.
