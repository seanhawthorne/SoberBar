Privacy Policy.

What our mailing-list form collects, why, and how we handle it.

Last updated: September 2026

Information We Collect

If you choose to use our mailing-list form, you provide an email address. We also use Google Analytics to collect limited website usage information, such as pages visited, approximate location, device and browser type, traffic source, and interactions with reservation, directions, email, and social links.

How the Form Works

When you submit the form, your browser opens a pre-filled email in your own mail app addressed to us — you send it yourself. The website itself does not store your email or transmit it to any server or third-party service.

How We Use Your Information

If you email us to join the list, we use your address only to send occasional updates about Sober Bar. We do not sell, rent, or share your information with anyone. Reply to any message to be removed, or email Sean@sipsoberbar.com anytime.

Cookies & Analytics

We use Google Analytics to understand how people find and use the site and to improve our marketing. Google may use cookies or similar technologies for this purpose. We do not send email addresses or other directly identifying form contents to Google Analytics. You can limit cookies in your browser settings or use Google's Analytics opt-out tools.

Contact

Questions? Email Sean@sipsoberbar.com.
